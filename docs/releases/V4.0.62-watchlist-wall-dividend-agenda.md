# V4.0.62 — mixed quote continuity, customized walls and dividend agenda

## Behavior
- A subscription quota rejection no longer closes an otherwise working Fugle trade connection. Discover unknown capacity through serial, acknowledged subscriptions; retain accepted trades, prioritize held symbols, and release old slots before replacements. No fixed account-tier cap or extra polling/socket.
- If even one subscription cannot be accepted, retain bounded reconnect/backoff. A previous transport retry cannot prevent later quota discovery. Coverage remains degraded and the external wall labels limited rows as last valid quotes, with a visible count.
- New watches append to explicit wall selections while reusing the existing model, fields, font/effect settings, dimensions and layout. Empty selection retains its existing “all” meaning. Hydration does not overwrite restored selections. Switching source also reuses presentation settings.
- The dividend calendar's daily agenda lists all registered/imported events on the selected date, including last purchase, ex-dividend, record and payment dates. Agenda ignores month-dot visibility filters and has no three-event truncation; a Today action restores today's date/month. Coverage is labeled and does not claim complete exchange-wide announcements.
- Forecast plans remain read-only calendar events until the existing explicit actual-payment confirmation. No ledger or canonical finance formula changes.

## Validation
- Real provider/fake transport: partial accepted subscriptions survive a quota error and deliver subsequent trades; cold bulk denial serially discovers capacity, including after earlier retries; replacement waits for unsubscribe ACK; larger capacities remain supported.
- Existing V4.0.57 recovery suite retains increasing backoff for zero-coverage quota failures.
- Actual MarketRuntime hook: held > watch > transient research ownership and removal isolation.
- Customized wall membership changes preserve every presentation property; full daily agenda includes 14 events across seven instruments without mutating plans.
- Inherited gates, TypeScript checks and Android CI are required for this release.

The original phone's upgrade white screen/jank and its actual Fugle error response still require device acceptance; neither the screenshot nor simulated transport establishes the sole on-device cause.
