# TF Asset V4.0.45 — SaiETF 2.0.2 is the market price authority

SaiETF 2.0.2 (source repo `locopy0121-hub/Saietf_V1.0.1`) is the
single authoritative market update system. The TF Asset application preserves
its account ledger, UI, Settings and finance/portfolio logic but must never
run a second independent price arbitration or fabricate new live ticks.

## Ownership
- Streaming subscription and provider adapters: SaiETF Fugle WebSocket, TWSE MIS, Yahoo.
- Price acceptance / market source order / exchange timestamp / sequence / circuit
  breaker: frozen SaiETF MarketDataCenter, MarketArbitrator, MemoryMarketStore.
- Native Android presentation adapter: read StateFlow snapshots, annotate freshness
  without changing the price or writing to the immutable Market Core.
- TF Asset React Native: show the native center-selected quote and report an
  updated tick only if the accepted exchange clock or Fugle sequence advanced.
- Persistence: original market timestamps retained; prior-session quote may
  remain as last known display but is not current session live quote or coverage.
- Every downstream consumer must read the MarketRuntime's native-selected quotes:
  dashboard, holdings, market wall, Mini, individual instrument, charts, Widget,
  Monitor and portfolio valuation. Do not route another TF-only provider into them.

## Protected
Settings/Control Center UI, Ledger, immutable SaiETF Market Core,
Android identifiers/upgrade migration and account data remain unchanged.

## Evidence
- `scripts/v4_0_45-saietf-authority.test.ts`
- inherited `scripts/v4_0_44-saietf-sync.test.ts`
- TypeScript/finance/UI/native gates, Android QA APK, official market real-device
  post-install check. CI success alone cannot prove real-time source availability.
- SaiETF and TF Asset Keystore are separate: Fugle API key must exist in TF Asset
  independently. HEALTHY / READY is not evidence of a new tick.
