# V4.0.51 QA — ETF 基本資料與成分異動

Base: V4.0.50 `ec12e3078efc7292dbeac9be1ab0ef2edac417b0`.
Backup: `backup-v4.0.50-pre-etf-profile-20261013`.

## Scope and acceptance

1. ETF profile tab must show the reference's fund fields, while stock profiles retain the existing company fields. Twenty-three fields cover names, identifier, exchange, issuer, dates, currency, size, close, NAV, premium, targets, geography, costs, payout frequency, yield, custodian, manager, separate tracking/benchmark indexes and strategy.
2. Retrieve TWSE fund basic data and MoneyDJ fund reference data on demand. Official fields take priority. Preserve provider units, dates and field-level sources. Missing total fees remain missing; do not insert zero or infer a benchmark from a tracking index.
3. Cache by symbol and persist locally. A late response must not enter a different selected symbol. Failed refreshes retain dated reference data. These references do not alter canonical LIVE prices or accounting.
4. Existing constituent cache retains one previous validated snapshot. Compare complete, same-source, same-basis snapshots; incomplete or changed-source data gets a baseline notice. New/removed/weight-change notices include symbol, name, before/after weight and comparison dates. First load is a baseline, not a flood of additions.
5. Replacement pairs require explicit source-provided pairs, matching added/removed symbols and a supported official announcement URL. Mere simultaneous additions/removals are not paired. When no such announcement is supplied, display that pairing and effective date await official confirmation.
6. Restore comparison baselines through the existing constituent persistence. Same-day identical responses retain notices; older responses cannot replace newer snapshots. Large change lists expand on demand.
7. MoneyDJ comparison completeness requires a matching all-holdings heading and recognition of every stock row. Explicit futures and Taiwan index option contracts are outside the stock scope. Unknown rows remain partial. Legacy MoneyDJ caches and backend payloads without completeness evidence do not produce removal notices; establish a new verified baseline first.
